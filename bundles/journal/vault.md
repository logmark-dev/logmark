# Daily journal

## Purpose
A vault for life - what I'm thinking, deciding, noticing, working on. No project, no topic. Just me over time.

## How agents should help
Surface patterns in what I've been writing. Highlight recurring themes, decisions I keep deferring, ideas I haven't returned to. Don't summarize my days - I write to think, not to recap.

## Don't surface
Anything tagged #scratch, anything that feels like a draft I deleted on purpose.
