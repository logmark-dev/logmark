# Research notes

## Purpose
A vault for chasing a question - paper notes, sources, ideas you're connecting, gaps you're filling. Inquiry happens here; what gets shipped elsewhere.

## Scope
INCLUDE: reading notes, paper summaries, hypotheses, methodology decisions, gaps in the literature, ideas connecting sources. EXCLUDE: unrelated work, calendar logistics, scratch.

## How agents should help
Connect new captures to prior work in the same line of inquiry. Surface contradictions between what I'm reading and what I've captured before. Flag questions and gaps I haven't followed up on. NotebookLM-style synthesis is useful; verbatim summaries are not.

## Don't surface
Routine literature captures unless they tie to an open question; preprints I haven't read end-to-end.
